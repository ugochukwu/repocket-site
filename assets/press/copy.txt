Repocket: short and long copy
Updated 12 October 2026

Short description
Spend from whatever's handy. Repocket keeps the tally, and on payday it shows you exactly what to move back.

Long description
Repocket helps you settle, between pockets, accounts or the people you hold money for. Spend from whatever's handy, log it in a line, and Repocket keeps a running tally of which pocket covered which. On payday it shows exactly what to move back, and one tap marks it settled. Made independently by Michel Onwordi (Mugoworks) in Germany. No bank login and no account; entries sync through your private iCloud.
