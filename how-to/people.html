Guide 2 · Money you hold for people

Holding money for people? Give each one a pocket.

Say one bank account holds money for twenty people, and a spreadsheet tracks whose share is whose. Repocket can carry that tally for you.

Your ledger stays yours. Nobody else uses it, and each person’s share is simply one of your own pockets.

Follow the money

One cover, start to square.

Six panels, one line in Repocket, and everyone ends up square.

  1. Pockets
    1

    Nia, everyone else, and My money.

    Nia gets a pocket, and so does everyone else on your list. Add one called My money for your own share.

  2. Cover
    2

    Nia needs it fast. My money steps in.

    Her share is sitting in the bank, but she needs it fast. So you send it from your own money. Quick and kind.

  3. Log
    3

    Paid from My money, covering for Nia.

    Log it in one line and Repocket remembers. No spreadsheet cell to hunt for.

  4. To settle
    4

    My money covered Nia. Back to My money.

    To settle lists Nia’s $250.00 under Back to My money, until you move her share across.

  5. Settle
    5

    Nia’s share moves across. Tap Mark settled.

    When you are ready, move Nia’s share over to your own money in the bank, then tap Mark settled.

  6. Square
    =

    Everything is square.

    Nia has her money, yours is back home, and the spreadsheet gets the day off.
