Guide 1 · Everyday pockets

Spend from any pocket. Settle later.

You keep pockets at your bank. Some days the handy one is not the one you meant, and that is fine. Repocket keeps it straight.

  1. 01
    Pockets

    Keep pockets at your bank.

    Vacation, Groceries, Home: use the names your bank uses. Your first cover opens on starter pockets, so tap one or type your own.

  2. 02
    Cover

    At the till, Vacation covers for Groceries.

    Groceries is a little light today, so the Vacation card pays. That is a cover, and it is exactly what pockets are for.

  3. 03
    Log

    Log it in a line.

    Tap +, type the amount, then Paid from Vacation, Covering for Groceries. Save. Done before the bags are packed.

  4. 04
    To settle

    See what’s left to settle.

    To settle adds up every cover in one number, so nothing slips your mind. Vacation covered Groceries, $45.50.

  5. 05
    Settle

    Move it back. Tap Mark settled.

    On payday, move $45.50 back to Vacation in your bank app. Then open the pair and tap Mark settled. Using the widget? Its Settled button does it in one tap.

  6. 06
    Square

    Everything is square.

    That is the whole loop. Spend from any pocket, tell Repocket, and it will remember.
