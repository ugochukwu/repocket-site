Repocket

Press kit

Repocket is an iPhone app that tracks money borrowed between a person's own bank pockets (N26 Spaces, DKB Unterkonten, Revolut Vaults, Monzo Pots). Log a borrow in one sentence; Repocket derives who owes whom and what to move back. One-time €3.99 unlock after a 7-day trial. No subscription, no bank connection, no account.

Facts

  • Platform: iPhone, iOS 18+. English and German.
  • Price: free 7-day trial, then €3.99 once. Lean free tier after trial.
  • Privacy: no backend, no analytics, private iCloud sync.
  • Maker: mugoworks, Germany.

Boilerplate

Repocket is the ledger for what you borrowed from yourself. You split money into pockets at the bank, then pay for groceries with the vacation card. The bank records one card; Repocket records the debt between your pockets and tells you what to move back.
