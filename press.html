About Repocket

Repocket helps you settle up with yourself. Spend from whichever bank pocket is handy, log it in a line, and Repocket keeps a running tally of which pocket owes which. On payday it shows exactly what to move back, and one tap marks it settled. Made independently by Michel Onwordi (Mugoworks) in Germany. No bank login and no account; entries sync through your private iCloud.
